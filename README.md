# CIVORA

### People. Intelligence. Action.

> An AI-powered community action platform that turns scattered community problems into coordinated human action.

## Overview

CIVORA is a social-impact platform designed to connect citizens, volunteers, NGOs, organizations, and community resources around real-world problems.

Instead of treating every report as an isolated complaint, CIVORA uses intelligent analysis to understand what is happening, assess urgency, identify related reports, find suitable people and resources, coordinate human action, and measure the resulting impact.

The core philosophy is simple:

**AI coordinates. Humans act. Communities create the impact.**

---

## The Problem

Many community problems are not caused by a lack of people willing to help.

The challenge is coordination.

A person may report an urgent problem, while nearby volunteers, NGOs, hospitals, organizations, or useful resources remain unaware of it.

At the same time, many individual reports may actually represent one larger recurring community problem.

CIVORA creates a digital coordination layer between these disconnected signals.
