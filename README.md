# 🌍 What is CIVORA?

CIVORA is a social-impact platform designed to connect **citizens, volunteers, organizations, NGOs and community resources** around real-world problems.

The platform focuses on the part that traditional reporting systems often miss:

> **What happens after someone reports a problem?**

CIVORA uses intelligent analysis to understand reports, assess urgency, identify related community reports, discover suitable resources and coordinate people who can help.

The goal is not to replace human decision-making with AI.

The goal is to use AI to make **human action faster, smarter and more coordinated.**

### The core philosophy

> **AI coordinates. Humans act. Communities create the impact.**

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Communities face thousands of everyday problems:

- Unsafe roads
- Medical assistance requests
- Food shortages
- Elderly assistance
- Accessibility problems
- Infrastructure issues
- Environmental concerns
- Shelter requirements
- Emergency situations

The problem is often not the complete absence of people willing to help.

The problem is **coordination**.

A citizen may report an issue while a volunteer is nearby.

An NGO may have the required resources while not knowing that the problem exists.

Several citizens may independently report the same issue without realizing that they are describing one larger community problem.

This creates fragmented information and delayed action.
